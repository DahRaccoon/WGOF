Tournament Recap

Ryan Goes Back-to-Back — and Takes the Cup Lead

Ryan Edwards followed his Edwards Invitational playoff breakthrough with another WGOF victory, posting an 89 (+16) at the Flynnvitational. Host Keith Flynn kept it close all day and finished just one stroke back at 90, while Sterling Weed secured third with a 94. The win gives Ryan 4,000 season points and the Cup lead, 125 clear of Kyle Merkley, heading toward the final championship.

One Stroke Short

Keith Nearly Wins His Own Flynnvitational

Keith Flynn fired a 43 on the back nine, the best closing nine in the field, but his 90 left him one stroke behind Ryan. The runner-up finish was worth 1,050 WGOF points and kept him within striking distance in the Cup race.

Podium Finish

Sterling Grabs Third — and 825 Points

Sterling Weed carded a 94, with a front-nine 49 that tied Kyle and a strong 45 on the back. His third-place showing earned 825 points and gives Steady Eddy something to build on going into the championship.

Cup Standings

Kyle Slips to Second in Season Race

Kyle Merkley entered the Flynnvitational with a 250-point lead on Ryan. After posting a 97, Kyle collected 725 points. He now sits second at 3,875, just 125 behind Ryan with the season title still in play.

Final Tournament Ahead

Devon Has a Championship Comeback in Mind

Devon Jarvis struggled through a 104 at Hidden Valley and now sits third in the Cup with 3,650 points. The leaderboard may have shifted, but he remains only 350 points off Ryan heading toward the finale.
